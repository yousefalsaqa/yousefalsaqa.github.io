Two kinds of system, one job

Half my work is physical: a manikin that has to respond correctly while a nursing student is holding it, a vehicle deciding every 100 milliseconds, a rover that has to survive its own drivetrain. The other half is software with the same constraint — a scoring engine running during a live match, an agent holding state across weeks, a game where the physics is the mechanic.

They are the same job: find where the thing comes apart, and get there first. I graduated from Queen's in mechatronics and robotics engineering in 2025.

Have a system that has to hold?
